I have wanted to start this blog for a number of years, but never quite found the time. This is a place for notes, ideas, experiments, and small observations that I want to write down before they disappear into the usual fog of tabs, conversations, paper margins, and half-remembered links.

The main motivation is simple: to keep things somewhere I can find them again. Some posts may be short pointers to useful resources, some may be longer reflections on research, teaching, software, visual computing, or academic life, and some may simply be a record of something I tried and what I learned from it.

If any of it is useful to someone else, even better.

Everything here is offered as-is, with no warranty. These posts are working notes rather than formal documentation, and they may contain incomplete thoughts, rough edges, outdated details, or opinions that change as I learn more.
